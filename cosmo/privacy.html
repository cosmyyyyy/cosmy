Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

To provide our services, Cosmo may collect and store the following data:

  • User IDs and Server IDs.
  • Message content (only when explicitly required for a command, like tags or custom responses).
  • Usage statistics to improve performance.

2. How We Use Data

Stored data is used exclusively for the bot's functionality (e.g., remembering your level, custom settings, or moderation logs). We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties.

3. Data Retention

Data is stored as long as the bot is present on your server. If the bot is removed, server-specific data may be retained for a short period to allow for restoration, unless a manual deletion is requested.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your data. To do so, please join our Support Server and contact the development team. Note that deleting data may result in the loss of progress (e.g., levels or custom configurations).

5. External Links

Our bot and website may contain links to Discord or other third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external platforms.
